Scenario representation & rebase — wayframe#87

Question this demo answers: is a Scenario a sparse list of deltas layered over Baseline, or a fully materialized copy? And when a delta's target Baseline milestone or phase is concurrently modified or deleted underneath it, does the model rebase silently, orphan the delta invisibly, or surface a conflict? Two independent editors act on the same world below — a Baseline (the plan's live current state, per #85) and a Scenario (a set of deltas on top of it) — and the Resolved Scenario View shows what a collaborator looking at this Scenario would actually see right now.

Push both sides through the awkward orders below: edit a field the scenario already overrode, delete a milestone a scenario delta still targets, delete one a scenario already hid, and add a scenario-only item that depends on something Baseline later removes.
