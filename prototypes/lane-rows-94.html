Lane Rows & vertical allocation

wayframe#94 (t20). Today, one lane auto-stacks overlapping duration pills into sub-rows (stack-intervals.ts) and use-auto-lane-height shrinks every lane's height by dividing viewport space evenly across the raw lane count. This prototype answers: how does a new, explicit Lane Row (row 2, 3, … — created only when an item is assigned to it, gone again once empty) coexist with that? Is it one allocator or two, and does assigning an item to a row override automatic stacking or merely seed it?
