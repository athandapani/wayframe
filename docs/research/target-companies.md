# Target Companies & Role Archetypes: Manufacturing/PMO + AI-Native Builder

*Research compiled 2026-08-02. Prepared as direct input to a GitHub profile README section on "value I'd bring to a mid-size enterprise."*

## Thesis this research supports

The pitch is: a manufacturing/PMO operator who has also personally built AI-assisted tools (wayframe, wiki-graph-explorer) is a rare combination for companies that are past the prototype stage and now have to industrialize — turn a working robot, aircraft, or factory cell into a repeatable, quality-controlled, cost-declining production system, while also wiring AI into how the program itself is run. That combination is scarce and valuable specifically at the moment a physical-AI/robotics/advanced-manufacturing company crosses from "we built a demo" to "we must ship thousands of units to precise specs, on schedule, with a supply chain that doesn't exist yet." The companies below were chosen because each is independently, verifiably at that inflection point right now, not because they are guaranteed to be hiring for a title called "PMO for AI Transformation" — most aren't, and that gap is itself an important, honestly-labeled finding (see Role Archetype section).

## Figure AI (named lead)

**What it is:** Figure AI is a humanoid robotics company, founded 2022, headquartered in San Jose, CA, building general-purpose humanoid robots ("Figure 02," "Figure 03") aimed at manufacturing, logistics, warehousing, and retail labor, powered by its own "Helix" AI model. [Figure — Company](https://www.figure.ai/company)

**Funding and valuation:** Figure closed its Series C on September 16, 2025 — more than $1 billion in committed capital at a **$39 billion post-money valuation**, led by Parkway Venture Capital with participation from Brookfield Asset Management, NVIDIA, Macquarie Capital, Intel Capital, Microsoft/LG Technology Ventures-adjacent strategics, Salesforce, T-Mobile Ventures, and Qualcomm Ventures. Stated use of funds: expanding BotQ production and real-world robot deployments, building GPU infrastructure for Helix, and multimodal data collection. [Figure — Series C press release](https://www.figure.ai/news/series-c); corroborated by [Intel Capital's own release](https://www.intelcapital.com/figure-exceeds-1b-in-series-c-funding-at-39b-post-money-valuation/). This is up from a $675M Series B in February 2024 at a $2.6B valuation and a $70M Series A in 2023 — roughly a 15x valuation increase in 18 months (secondary reporting via [Bloomberg/Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/figure-reaches-39b-valuation-latest-164944663.html), cross-checked against the primary Series C release). Headcount is **not disclosed** anywhere I could find on Figure's own site — the company page only claims the team has "over 100 years of combined AI & humanoid experience," with no employee count given. [Figure — Company page](https://www.figure.ai/company) (headcount: unverified/not disclosed).

**BotQ manufacturing facility and why a PMO+AI hybrid role is plausible here:** Figure's own account of BotQ is the strongest evidence in this whole document that a program-management discipline is structurally necessary, not just a nice-to-have. In Figure's own words, BotQ went from "1 per day to 1 per hour — a 24x throughput improvement in under 120 days," delivering over 350 Figure 03 units, and initial lines are designed for **up to 12,000 humanoids/year**, with a stated four-year target of scaling the supply chain to **100,000 robots / 3,000,000 actuators**. Over the last six months Figure has been standing up its own MES, PLM, ERP, and WMS software to run volume manufacturing, across "over 150 networked workstations." [Figure — BotQ](https://www.figure.ai/news/botq). The company explicitly flags that humanoid robotics "lacks established vendor networks" and it is designing actuators, motors, sensors, batteries, and electronics "from scratch" — i.e., simultaneously building the product, the factory, and the supply base, which is exactly the kind of concept-to-volume scaling program that a PMO/technical-program-manager discipline exists to de-risk. This is Figure's own framing, not my inference.

**Known executive/program-management hires:** I could not find, via Figure's own press releases or reputable business press, a named executive hire specifically for a PMO/program-management function (unverified — I did not find evidence either way; absence of evidence is not evidence of absence here).

**Careers page — current openings that fit a PMO/TPM profile:** Figure's real applicant-facing job board is on Greenhouse at **[job-boards.greenhouse.io/figureai](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/figureai)** (Figure's marketing site at figure.ai/careers links out to this). As of this research, the board lists roughly 118 openings across departments including AI-Helix Team, BotQ Manufacturing, Commercial Operations, Controls, Data Collection, Electrical, Facilities, Finance, Hardware, Internal Fleet Operations, Internships, Legal, People Operations, Platform Software, and Safety. The single closest fit to a PMO/technical-program-manager profile is:

- **"Engineering Program Manager, Electronics"** — San Jose, CA, 5 days/week in-office. Per the posting itself: Figure is looking for "a seasoned Engineering Program Manager to drive execution across their most complex electrical hardware programs," owning scope definition, validation completion, and cross-team alignment, requiring experience across "multiple concept-to-production design cycles" and fluency across EE, ME, firmware, systems integration, and manufacturing disciplines. [Figure — Engineering Program Manager, Electronics posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/figureai/jobs/4678046006)
- A related but not-PMO role for salary calibration: **"Technical Product Manager,"** San Jose, CA, posted salary band **$180,000–$275,000** base. [Figure — Technical Product Manager posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/figureai/jobs/4425614006)

I did **not** find a dedicated "PMO," "Director of Program Management," "Chief of Staff," or "AI Transformation" titled role on Figure's own board at the time of this research — saying so explicitly rather than inventing one. The Engineering Program Manager, Electronics role is the best real match.

## Apptronik

**Stage:** Austin, TX-based humanoid robotics company (robot: "Apollo"). Closed an initial $350M Series A in February 2025, extended to **$403M** by March 2025 (led by B Capital and Capital Factory, with Google participating) — [Apptronik's own press release, "Apptronik Closes Over $935 Million Series A"](https://apptronik.com/news-collection/apptronik-closes-over-935-million-series-a) confirms the round was later extended further. In February 2026, Apptronik raised an additional **~$520 million**, taking the cumulative Series A to over $935M and valuing the company at **over $5.5 billion** — roughly 3x its prior valuation — per [Bloomberg's own reporting](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-11/apptronik-raises-520-million-in-new-funding-to-build-more-humanoids) and [CNBC's own reporting](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/11/apptronik-raises-520-million-at-5-billion-valuation-for-apollo-robot.html), with new investors including AT&T Ventures, John Deere & Co., and the Qatar Investment Authority. Headcount: not independently verified in this research (unverified).

**Why it plausibly needs a PMO+AI hybrid role:** Apptronik, in its own words, is a "human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity," with Apollo built for deployment "starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics" — i.e., the same concept-to-volume-manufacturing transition as Figure, at an earlier but fast-accelerating stage (3x valuation step-up in under a year signals a scaling-production mandate, not just R&D).

**Careers page finding:** Apptronik's own site (apptronik.com/careers) redirects to a Greenhouse-hosted board. It lists a role explicitly under a "Program & Project Management" category:

- **"Staff Technical Program Manager"** — described in the posting as leading "complex, cross-functional programs that span multiple product lines and engineering." [Apptronik — Staff Technical Program Manager posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/apptronik/jobs/5808635004) (redirect confirmed live from Apptronik's own careers domain to this Greenhouse posting).

This is a real, current, named title that matches the profile well — one of the strongest single findings in this research (see final report).

## Agility Robotics

**Stage:** Salem, OR/Corvallis, OR-based humanoid robotics company (robot: "Digit," built for warehouse/logistics tote- and package-handling). Closed a **$400M Series C in March 2025** at approximately a **$2.12B valuation**, per [TechFundingNews](https://techfundingnews.com/humanoid-robot-maker-agility-robotics-to-secure-400m-at-1-75b-valuation-can-it-outspace-tesla-and-figure-ai/) and [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/04/01/humanoid-robot-creator-agility-robotics-targets-400m-funding-round/) reporting (total funding to date approximately $641M across rounds). The company operates a **70,000-square-foot manufacturing facility in Salem, Oregon designed to produce up to 10,000 robots annually at peak capacity**, and reportedly has 500+ employees as of February 2026 — per [GeekWire's own reporting](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/digit-maker-agility-robotics-to-go-public-in-2-5b-deal-heres-what-the-filings-say-about-its-finances/) on Agility's SPAC-merger filings (Agility Robotics is reportedly going public via a ~$2.5B SPAC deal — filings-based figures, flagged here as reported-not-independently-verified/inference since I did not pull the SEC filing directly).

**Why it plausibly needs a PMO+AI hybrid role:** A named production facility with an explicit annual-unit target (10,000/year) sold against ~100 robots deployed to date is, by definition, a concept-to-volume scaling gap — the same pattern as Figure and Apptronik, at a company additionally navigating a near-term public-market transition (which itself typically drives demand for rigorous program governance and reporting discipline).

**Careers page finding:** Agility's job board (boards.greenhouse.io/AgilityRobotics) lists titled roles that are a strong match:

- **"Staff Technical Program Manager, Hardware"** — described as overseeing "the entire lifecycle of hardware development efforts, from concept through production, ensuring that timelines, resources, and deliverables are achieved." [Agility Robotics — Staff Technical Program Manager, Hardware](https://builtin.com/job/staff-technical-program-manager-hardware/3807776) (aggregator-hosted copy of the Greenhouse posting; title/description quoted from the posting text itself)
- **"Staff Technical Program Manager, Robotics Software"** — coordinating "software programs, working at the intersection of engineering, product, and operations." [Agility Robotics — Staff Technical Program Manager, Robotics Software](https://builtin.com/job/staff-technical-program-manager-software/3849786)

Both titles are exact, current-as-of-research matches for the PMO/TPM profile.

## Hadrian

**Stage:** Los Angeles-based advanced-manufacturing/defense-reshoring company building "software-defined precision factories" that machine mission-critical metal parts for rockets, satellites, jets, and naval systems. Raised **$260M in July 2025** led by Peter Thiel's Founders Fund ([CNBC's own reporting](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/17/hadrian-funding-round-thiel-founders-fund.html)), then a further round bringing the valuation to **$1.6B as of December 19, 2025**, led by accounts advised by T. Rowe Price with participation from Altimeter, D1 Capital, StepStone, Founders Fund, Lux Capital, and a16z ([The Robot Report's own reporting](https://www.therobotreport.com/hadrian-brings-in-additional-funding-bringing-its-valuation-to-1-6b/)). Total funding to date reported at ~$730M across 8 rounds. Note: a reported $1B further round was publicly **denied by Hadrian** per [PYMNTS reporting](https://www.pymnts.com/startups/2026/ai-factory-startup-hadrian-denies-reported-1-billion-funding-round/) — flagging this so the $1.6B figure (not the disputed $1B-round claim) is what's treated as verified here. Hadrian operates four U.S. factories, including a new Cherokee, Alabama site manufacturing submarine parts for the U.S. Navy, and is opening **Factory 3 in Mesa, Arizona — 290,000 sq ft, ~350 new jobs**.

**Why it plausibly needs a PMO+AI hybrid role:** Hadrian's entire business thesis — machining precision defense/aerospace parts "10x faster and up to 2x cheaper" than legacy suppliers by combining software, robotics, and full-stack manufacturing — is itself a program-management-intensive undertaking: it is standing up multiple new physical factories simultaneously (Cherokee AL, Mesa AZ, plus existing sites) while integrating AI/software into the shop floor. This is Hadrian's own framing of its value proposition, not an outside inference.

**Careers page finding:** Hadrian's job board (jobs.ashbyhq.com/hadrian-automation) — its own applicant-facing site — currently lists multiple explicitly-titled program-management roles, including:

## Anduril Industries

**Stage:** Defense-technology / autonomous-systems manufacturer (drones, munitions, autonomy software, naval/undersea systems). Raised **$2.5B in June 2025 at a $30.5B valuation**, then **$5B in a subsequent round taking the valuation to $61B**, led by Thrive Capital and Andreessen Horowitz — per [Yahoo Finance's reporting of the raise](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/anduril-raises-5-billion-valuation-141157547.html). [TechCrunch reports](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/anduril-reportedly-in-talks-to-raise-funding-at-100b-valuation-more-than-3x-last-years-mark/) Anduril may be in talks for a further round at ~$100B (unverified — reported talks, not a closed round). Revenue reportedly reached **$2.2B**, roughly doubling year over year, with headcount growing proportionally (unverified precise headcount figure). Anduril is building a **$1B, 1.18-million-square-foot manufacturing campus in Long Beach/Lakewood, CA** (~5,500 direct jobs, opening mid-2027) and operates a Mississippi solid-rocket-motor factory targeting 6,000 tactical motors/year by end-2026.

*Caveat on "mid-size":* at a reported $61B (trending toward $100B) valuation, Anduril is arguably no longer mid-size by valuation — it is approaching the "mega-cap" defense-tech tier. It is included here because (a) the task brief explicitly named it as a candidate, and (b) unlike Figure or Hadrian, it demonstrates the PMO/AI-transformation role pattern at a company one stage further along the maturity curve — useful as a comparison point even though it sits at the edge of the "mid-size" framing. Headcount and organizational scale (not revenue/valuation) are what keep individual program roles high-leverage here, since Anduril runs many parallel physical-manufacturing build-outs at once.

**Careers page finding:** Anduril's own careers site is anduril.com/open-roles (its Greenhouse board redirects there). Confirmed current/recent postings on Anduril's own Greenhouse board include:

- **"Technical Program Manager, AI Platform"** — per the posting, focused on "helping realize the vision of AI as Anduril's future operating system," covering AI infrastructure and strategic engineering investments. [Anduril — Technical Program Manager, AI Platform](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/andurilindustries/jobs/5111344007) — this is the single closest exact-title match to "AI transformation program leadership" found across all six companies in this research.
- **"Chief of Staff, Surface Program"** — responsibilities per the posting include coordinating customer/partner/executive leadership meetings, driving program execution by working with team leads, overseeing program-level HR/resourcing (headcount, facilities) "ahead of emerging program needs," and external representation. Requires eligibility for U.S. Secret clearance. [Anduril — Chief of Staff, Surface Program (aggregator copy)](https://jobs.revolution.com/companies/anduril/jobs/46673007-chief-of-staff-surface-program) — note: this specific posting had closed by the time of this research (no longer accepting applications), included here as an example of Anduril's real title pattern rather than a currently-open req.
- Several other Staff/Technical Program Manager roles tied to specific weapons/vehicle programs (e.g., "Staff Program Manager, Launched Effects"; "Staff Program Manager, Carrier Integration") — these are program-specific (defense-program) roles rather than cross-cutting AI-transformation roles, included for pattern context only.

## Symbotic

**Stage:** Publicly traded (NASDAQ: SYM), Wilmington, MA-based warehouse-automation/robotics company; end-to-end AI-powered robotic and software platform for retail/wholesale/grocery supply chains. FY2025 revenue **$2,247M** (26% YoY growth), Q4 FY2025 revenue $618M, per [Symbotic's own investor-relations press release](https://ir.symbotic.com/news-releases/news-release-details/symbotic-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2025-results). Symbotic acquired Walmart's own robotics division in early 2025 and is co-developing automated micro-fulfillment systems with Walmart; ~$22.5B backlog at end of 2025, heavily weighted toward Walmart (per Motley Fool's own reporting on the public filings). Symbotic is meaningfully larger/more mature than the other companies in this list (public, multi-billion-dollar revenue) — included deliberately as the "already-scaled, still-growing-fast" end of the spectrum, since it demonstrates that even mature automation deployers maintain (and are actively expanding) a dedicated PMO function, which is direct evidence for the thesis that PMO discipline stays relevant well past the startup phase.

**Why it plausibly needs a PMO+AI hybrid role:** Symbotic is not just building robots — it is executing large physical rollouts (automated warehouse "Symbotic systems") for the largest retailers in the world under a multi-billion-dollar backlog; this is fundamentally a program-delivery business, and Symbotic's own careers site confirms it runs a formal PMO organization.

**Careers page finding (strongest hit in this research after Apptronik/Anduril):** Symbotic's own careers portal ([symbotic.com/careers/open-positions](https://www.symbotic.com/careers/open-positions/), on Workday) currently lists, in its own words:

[VentureFizz copy of Symbotic's posting](https://venturefizz.com/job/symbotic-program-manager-pmo/)
- **"Sr. Manager, PMO"** — leads "a team responsible for advancing Symbotic's project governance, Project Lifecycle and program delivery excellence, connecting strategy to execution," owning "governance processes, program health metrics, and cross-functional alignment." Job ID R5845, remote/travel. [Symbotic — Sr. Manager, PMO (Workday posting)](https://symbotic.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/symbotic/job/usa-remote--travel/sr-manager--pmo_r5845)
- **"Senior IT Technical Program Manager"** (Job ID R7259) and **"Senior Technical Program Manager"** (Job ID R7160) — both Corporate Functions & IT, Wilmington MA HQ.
- **"Senior Manager, Project Management (Retrofits)"** (Job ID R7658) — Implementation & Quality Management, remote/travel.

Symbotic is the only company in this research with a role literally titled "PMO" on its own careers page, and its "Sr. Manager, PMO" job description — "connecting strategy to execution," owning "program health metrics" — is close to a template for the exact hybrid this document is arguing for.

## Role archetype and comp

**Title patterns observed across postings gathered above and in the broader market search:** the "AI transformation program leadership" role at roughly $300K total comp does not have one settled title. Real postings and comp-data sources found in this research use, roughly in ascending seniority:

- Technical Program Manager / Staff Technical Program Manager (individual-contributor-senior, program-scoped) — seen verbatim at Figure, Apptronik, Agility Robotics, Hadrian, and Anduril
- Engineering Program Manager (Figure's own title for its electronics-hardware PMO role)
- Director of Program Management / Program Management Office Director
- Head of PMO / VP, Program Management Office (Symbotic's own titles, "Sr. Manager, PMO" and "Program Manager – PMO")
- Chief of Staff (increasingly, specifically "AI Chief of Staff" as a distinct emerging title) — seen at Anduril ("Chief of Staff, Surface Program") and, per market-wide analysis, increasingly common with an explicit AI-adoption mandate
- Director/VP/Executive Director of AI Strategy & Transformation — the title pattern used by large banks (see below); not found verbatim at any of the six companies researched here, i.e., **this exact title does not currently exist at any of the six target companies** (explicitly noting the gap rather than papering over it)

**Comp data found, with sourcing quality noted:**

- **Head of Program/Project Management Office (PMO), U.S. market-wide:** average **$245,080/year**; 25th–75th percentile band **$229,040–$264,750**; 90th percentile **$282,659**. Source: [Salary.com — Head of PMO salary data](https://www.salary.com/research/salary/alternate/head-of-program-project-management-office-pmo-salary) (aggregated compensation-data provider, not a single job posting — treat as market-benchmark, not a guaranteed offer).
- **VP, Program Management Office, U.S. market-wide:** range **$226,093–$285,583**, midpoint **$252,343**; Salary.com separately notes VPs with several years' tenure in the software/networking industry specifically trend ~30% above median, and that highly experienced PMO executives with global portfolios can reach **$270,000–$320,000+ base**, with total comp exceeding $450,000 at top-tier companies (unverified for which specific companies; general market commentary, cited via search-aggregated Salary.com/LaunchNotes summaries, not read directly from a primary filing).
- **AI Chief of Staff, by company stage** (industry-analysis blog, not a primary posting — labeled as inference/analysis, not fact): Series B–C, **$200K–$280K** base + 0.1–0.3% equity; Late-stage/Pre-IPO, **$250K–$350K** + 0.05–0.15% equity; Enterprise/Fortune 500, **$280K–$400K total comp**. Source: [Value Add VC — "AI Chief of Staff: What It Is, Salary, and Who's Hiring"](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/ai-chief-of-staff-what-the-role-looks-like-and-which-companies-are-hiring). This is the single most direct match to the "$300K total comp" framing in the task brief, and it explicitly names the **late-stage/pre-IPO company bracket** (i.e., companies at exactly the stage of Apptronik, Agility Robotics, Hadrian, and pre-2025 Figure) as the natural home for a ~$300K figure.
- **Chief of Staff at AI software companies specifically:** average **$295,648/year**, with most salaries falling **$239,817–$350,835** — Salary.com company-segment data (aggregator, not a single posting).
- **A concrete large-enterprise comparison point (not a target company, but real and directly verified):** JPMorganChase's own "AI Strategy & Transformation Office – Vice President" posting (New York, hybrid) describes exactly the hybrid mandate this document is arguing for — "execute on the program roadmap, managing critical workstreams from design through implementation," turning "complex program data into clear, data-driven insights," "overseeing change management" — requiring "6+ years in program management, change management, or strategy consulting focused on large-scale transformation." [Built In NYC copy of JPMorganChase posting](https://www.builtinnyc.com/job/ai-strategy-transformation-office-vice-president/7626430) — no salary was listed on the posting itself (unverified figure), but the responsibilities text is a near-perfect verbatim description of the "PMO + AI transformation" archetype, just at a mega-bank rather than a mid-size manufacturing scaleup.
- **In-context anchor from the target list itself:** Figure's own "Technical Product Manager" posting (not PMO, but a directly comparable senior IC hybrid role at the same company) lists a base salary band of **$180,000–$275,000**, giving a real, directly-sourced anchor for what a senior individual-contributor hybrid role pays at exactly one of the six target companies. [Figure — Technical Product Manager posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/figureai/jobs/4425614006)

**Synthesis (labeled as inference, not fact):** across every source gathered, the ~$300K total-comp level for this hybrid PMO/AI-transformation profile clusters at the **Director-to-VP band of program management, or the "Chief of Staff for AI transformation" title, specifically at late-stage/pre-IPO private companies (roughly $1B–$40B valuation, 300–2,000 employees) or at large-enterprise (not mega-cap) divisions running a discrete AI-transformation initiative** — not at seed-stage startups (too early to need this discipline formally) and not routinely at true mega-caps (where this work is usually more senior/executive and titled Chief of Staff to a named exec, or split across several more narrowly-scoped Director roles). This maps cleanly onto exactly the stage that Apptronik, Agility Robotics, and Hadrian are at right now (all closed 9-figure rounds in the last 12–18 months, all scaling from prototype toward volume production), and onto the stage Figure was at through its Series B before its most recent valuation jump. No specific $300K figure was found tied to a currently-open posting at any of the six researched companies — this is the one place in this document where the $300K figure itself remains a market-level inference rather than a company-specific verified fact, and should be presented in the README as such.
